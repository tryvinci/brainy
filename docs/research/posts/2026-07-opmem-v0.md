# OpMem v0: operational memory correctness beyond LOCOMO recall

**Status:** Public research draft (Paper 1 track)  
**Date:** 2026-07-29  
**Systems:** Brainy (staging) · Mem0 Platform · Verbatim baseline  

## Headline

On the OpMem v0 suite — lifecycle suppression, correction stickiness, isolation,
staleness, and idempotency — **Brainy passes 12/12** while **Mem0 passes 9/12**
on the same staging counter-run. This is a production-correctness result, not a
LOCOMO J-score.

## Why this benchmark exists

LOCOMO / LongMemEval measure *retrieval accuracy*. They do not ask:

- Does a forgotten memory leak back after re-ingest?
- Does a correction stick when the old text reappears?
- Does tenant/subject isolation hold under lookalike content?
- Does the newest fact outrank a stale twin?

Those failures break real agents. OpMem makes each one a pass/fail fixture.

## Conversational recall (same-pin, not OpMem)

Under identical gpt-oss judge / top_k=30 / 1×30 LOCOMO smoke:

| System | Overall | multi-hop | temporal |
| --- | ---: | ---: | ---: |
| Brainy (peak) | 19/30 | 2–3/10 | 13/16 |
| Mem0 Platform | 12/30 | **6/10** | 2/16 |

Brainy leads overall/temporal on this pin; Mem0 leads multi-hop. Neither number
is Mem0’s blog ~92 (different judge, budget, suite). See
`docs/benchmarks/locomo-samepin-brainy-vs-mem0.md`.

## Architecture thesis (short)

Brainy separates **cognitive primitives** + **declarative vertical packs** from
the runtime. OpMem and marketing fixtures exercise lifecycle and precedence that
flat preference stores do not model.

## Limitations

- OpMem v0 is small (12 tasks); expanding systems beyond Mem0+verbatim is next.
- Marketing vertical moat assumes pack semantics; Mem0 fails those by design.
- Conversational SOTA claims require full LOCOMO under GPT-class identical pins.
