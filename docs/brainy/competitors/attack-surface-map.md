# Attack Surface Map: Brain-Like Taste Evolution

## Scope
This map identifies likely gaps in direct competitors when evaluated against the goal of simulating human taste evolution, belief adaptation, and identity-coherent reasoning.

## Cross-Market Weak Spots
- `inference` Most systems optimize retrieval quality and latency, not longitudinal taste maturation.
- `inference` Memory stacks often treat contradictions as retrieval concerns rather than explicit belief-life-cycle concerns.
- `inference` Few systems expose first-principles invariants as explicit immutable state.
- `inference` Identity appears represented as profile/persona data, but not as a formalized, auditable decision prior graph.

## Player-Specific Opportunities

### Mem0
- `inference` Opportunity to outperform on explicit cognitive state transitions (novice -> competent -> sharp -> elite) rather than generic memory persistence.

### Supermemory
- `inference` Opportunity to differentiate on introspection and reflection loops that transform outcome deltas into belief revision policies.

### Zep
- `inference` Opportunity to win on taste and conviction modeling layered above temporal graph memory.

### Letta
- `inference` Opportunity to provide stronger externalized taste-memory compression and evolution mechanisms than block-level persistence alone.

### Memobase
- `inference` Opportunity to generalize from user-profile memory into multi-layer belief and principle architectures.

### Cognee
- `inference` Opportunity to add explicit taste evolution and identity coherence primitives over graph pipelines.

## Strategic Direction for Brainy
- Build cognitive primitives as first-class objects, not emergent side effects.
- Encode belief revision and conflict reconciliation protocols explicitly.
- Distinguish stable principles from adaptive heuristics.
- Measure adaptation quality over time, not only one-shot retrieval accuracy.
