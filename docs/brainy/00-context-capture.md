# Brainy Context Capture

## Objective
Build a state-of-the-art memory system that emulates how smart humans learn, develop taste, and adapt beliefs, with marketing as the first proving domain and general applicability as the design constraint.

## Captured Ground Truth
- The system should not be reduced to extraction/reranking primitives.
- Higher-order cognition is primary: learning, taste formation, belief evolution, adaptation.
- High taste is associated with strong ability to identify what performs and what will perform.
- Taste formation is multi-factor: exposure volume, feedback quality, mentor priors, aesthetic principles, and identity.
- Novice -> competent -> sharp -> elite is the working maturity ladder.
- Stable layer: first principles and foundations.
- Adaptive layer: data-backed updates filtered through worldview.
- Signal-noise separation: pattern finding, zoom-out reasoning, repeated why-analysis.
- Belief change trigger: conviction failing to translate into outcomes.
- Reasoning style: ranked hypotheses over rigid probability-first modeling.
- Conflict handling: seek complementarity first, then experimentation.

## Program Constraints
- Keep unresolved deep cognition questions in a dedicated backlog.
- Continue execution in parallel tracks: competitor reverse engineering and cognition formalization.
- Benchmark against top direct competitors.
